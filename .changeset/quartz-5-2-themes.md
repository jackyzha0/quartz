Theme support for Obsidian themes: plugin factories may be async (Quartz awaits them); font loading is built in as `configuration.fonts` (formerly the `@quartz-community/quartz-fonts` plugin, whose config entry is applied automatically); callouts and transclusions render as `<div>` like in Obsidian; popovers carry Obsidian's `hover-popover`/`markdown-embed` classes.
