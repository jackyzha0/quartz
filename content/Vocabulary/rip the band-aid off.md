# Learning and Retention

- speech:: idiom
- definition:: To do something painful or unpleasant very quickly so that the discomfort, while typically more intense, is short-lived. "Band-Aid" is a brand of adhesive bandages, so it is sometimes capitalized in this usage.
- sentences:: You just need to rip the Band-Aid off and tell them the bad news.
