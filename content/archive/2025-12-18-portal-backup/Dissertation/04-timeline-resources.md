# Timeline and Resources

## Timeline Overview

The research timeline spans approximately one year with overlapping phases that balance focused investigation with opportunities for iteration.

### Phase 1: Foundation
Literature review and dataset construction establish foundations. Dataset audit ensures quality through multiple validation passes.

### Phase 2: Measurement
Systematic measurement of phrasing sensitivity across baseline models with extensive evaluation across multiple models and clinical scenarios.

### Phase 3: Causal Analysis
Mechanistic interpretability work with iteration between different intervention strategies as results reveal complexity in how linguistic variation propagates.

### Phase 4: Mitigation
Theoretical framework development and practical implementation through parameter-efficient fine-tuning with multiple training runs for refinement.

### Phase 5: Safety Evaluation
Integration of adapted models into realistic deployment scenarios with radiologist collaboration and iterative refinement.
