### Research Focus

This research addresses phrasing robustness challenges in medical AI:
- **Phrasing-Sensitive Failure** — models give different answers to equivalent questions
- **Misleading Explanation Effect** — explanation metrics may not align with correctness
- **Safety Considerations** — need for robust evaluation beyond standard metrics

### Deliverables
1. Measurement tools for systematic problem identification
2. Improved models with enhanced stability
3. Safety framework for clinical deployment
4. Open-source toolkit for reproducibility
5. Research papers for community dissemination

### Research Phases
- Measurement and causal analysis
- Model improvement
- Safety evaluation in realistic scenarios
- Ongoing dissemination
