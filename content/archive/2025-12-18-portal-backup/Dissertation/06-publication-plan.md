# Publication Plan

A plan for publishing research papers targeting computer vision, medical informatics, and machine learning venues.

## Publication Strategy

### Target Venues
- Top-tier computer vision conferences
- Medical imaging conferences
- Machine learning venues
- Medical informatics journals
- Healthcare AI conferences

### Approach

**Early Papers**: Establish the problem and show initial solutions across different communities.

**Core Methodology Papers**: Present deeper methodological contributions with expert validation.

**Benchmark and Evaluation Papers**: Focus on reproducibility and comprehensive evaluation with dataset releases.

### Target Impact

This publication plan aims to:

1. Establish the problem in top-tier venues
2. Present solutions across different communities
3. Enable reproducibility through benchmark and code releases
4. Validate clinical relevance through expert studies

The mix of venues ensures the work reaches computer vision researchers, medical imaging specialists, machine learning practitioners, and clinical informaticists.

## Timeline Considerations

Papers are planned to align with research phases, with earlier papers based on initial experiments and later papers having more comprehensive evaluations and human studies.

All planned papers will contribute to the dissertation while serving different audiences.
