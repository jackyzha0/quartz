# Thrust 4: Safe Clinical Deployment

> Goal: Integrate robust models into clinical workflows with calibrated abstention and safety guarantees.

## Deployment Philosophy

Accuracy alone is insufficient. Systems must know when to defer, communicate uncertainty, and avoid misleading explanations.

## Framework Components

- Selective prediction with uncertainty awareness
- Calibrated confidence thresholds
- Paraphrase-based consistency checking
- Human-in-the-loop integration

## Evaluation

- Safety metrics for critical findings
- Robustness under linguistic variation
- Trust calibration assessment

## Deliverables

- Triage policy with adjustable operating points
- Deployment guidelines
- Human evaluation study design

*Specific thresholds, performance targets, and implementation details reserved for publication.*
