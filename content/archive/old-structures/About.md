# About Binesh Kumar

I am a PhD student in the Secure and Assured Intelligent Learning (SAIL) Lab at the University of New Haven. My research explores how we can build multimodal language models that are:

- **Trustworthy** in high-stakes settings such as healthcare and critical infrastructure
- **Robust** against adversarial manipulation and data drift
- **Transparent** enough for practitioners to audit and improve

## Research Interests
- Multimodal and visual-language architectures
- Evaluation methodologies for long-context reasoning
- Safety, robustness, and governance of applied AI systems
- Healthcare applications of large foundation models
