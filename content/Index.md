# RobMed LLM Research Notes

PhD research on robustness of Medical Vision-Language Models for clinical applications.

---

## Research Overview

[[clinical-robust-vision-language-models|Clinically Robust Vision-Language Models for Diagnostic Applications]]

Medical VLMs can produce contradictory diagnoses when clinicians rephrase semantically equivalent questions—while explanations still appear correct. This research develops measurement frameworks, causal analysis tools, and mitigation strategies to make these models safer for clinical deployment.
